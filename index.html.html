Privacy Policy – AC Service Booking

Last updated: 28 October 2026

AC Service Booking helps customers request air-conditioning installation, maintenance and repair services, manage bookings, communicate about service appointments, and access related invoices and reviews. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the application and its related support and messaging features.

Information we may collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and account authentication or verification details.
  • Service and booking information: service address, selected service, preferred appointment time, booking status, issue descriptions, and photos you choose to upload.
  • Communications: support requests and messages related to arranging or completing a service.
  • Transactions and documents: payment status, invoice details, and records needed to manage a booking. Payment card or banking details may be handled by the relevant payment provider rather than stored by the application.
  • Reviews: ratings and feedback you submit after a service.
  • Technical information: limited device, diagnostic, and request information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts; schedule and manage bookings; assign technicians; provide customer support; send appointment, status, invoice, OTP, and other service-related notifications; process or reconcile payments; maintain records; handle ratings and complaints; prevent misuse; and improve the application.

Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only as reasonably needed to provide the service. For example, relevant booking and address details may be made available to the assigned technician and authorized administrators. Service providers may process information for hosting, database storage, email delivery, payment processing, or WhatsApp messaging. WhatsApp messages sent or received through the WhatsApp Business Platform are processed by Meta according to its applicable terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

WhatsApp and notifications

If WhatsApp messaging is enabled for your booking, your phone number and the message content or delivery information needed for that communication may be processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Do not send payment card details, passwords, or other sensitive information through chat. Notification delivery may depend on your contact details, device settings, provider availability, and the status of the integration.

Data retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate bookings and accounts, provide support, maintain invoices and business records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting the support contact for AC Service Booking. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping, security, or dispute-resolution purposes. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children

The application is intended for people who can independently arrange home services. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated date above will be revised when a new version is published.
